Northern Rwanda

Adventure beyond the trails. Comfort beyond the ordinary.

Wake up beneath Rwanda’s volcanoes and step into a world shaped by ancient forces and living landscapes.

At Grotta Resort, Northern Rwanda begins within, inside volcanic caves that have existed for thousands of years, and extends outward into a collection of the region’s most exceptional experiences.

Explore gorilla trekking, volcano hikes, mountain e-biking, caves, wildlife, and cultural journeys, all carefully curated to give you a complete and immersive stay.

Then return to fireside evenings, open views, and the quiet rhythm that defines Grotta Resort.

More than a place to stay, this is how you experience Northern Rwanda, fully.
