Rooms & Accommodation

Everything about our 26 rooms, amenities, and staying at Grotta Resort.
Grotta Resort has 26 physical rooms across 6 categories: Standard (3), King Room (6), King Volcano View (3), King Pool View (6), Deluxe (6), and Executive (2). Family Rooms are not additional rooms — they are created by combining two double rooms, and are available subject to configuration.
Every room at Grotta Resort includes: daily highland breakfast, access to the swimming pool, gym access, a guided property tour, and complimentary car cleaning. An evening bonfire is available on selected evenings, subject to weather, occupancy, and prior arrangement. Wi-Fi, air conditioning, in-room safe, smart TV, and bathrobes are also standard in all rooms.
No. Jacuzzi access is available in the King Volcano View, King Pool View, Family Room, and Deluxe Room. The Standard Room and King Room do not include Jacuzzi access.
The Family Room is a room configuration, not a separate room type. It is created by combining two double rooms into a connected suite with dedicated sleeping areas for parents and children. When a Family Room is booked, two double rooms are allocated to that booking. Availability is subject to configuration at time of booking. Family Rooms sleep up to 4 guests.

Dining

Our restaurant, Cave Café, Cocktail Bar, and meal plan options.
Grotta Resort has three dining and drinking venues: The Restaurant (fine dining, dinner from 6PM), the Cave Café (all-day café and rare spirits, 10AM–10PM), and the Cocktail Bar / Lounge (evenings from 5PM). Each has its own menu and atmosphere.
Yes. A highland breakfast is included with every room, every night. We also offer Half Board (breakfast + dinner, +$45 per adult per day) and Full Board (all meals, +$90 per adult per day) upgrades.
Reservations are strongly encouraged for The Restaurant, especially during weekends and peak periods. Walk-ins are welcome subject to availability. You can reserve via the website, by email, or by asking at reception on arrival.
Yes. Our kitchen accommodates most dietary requirements including vegetarian, vegan, gluten-free, and common allergies. Please inform us of any dietary needs at the time of booking or at least 24 hours before dining so our team can prepare accordingly.
Outside catering for food is not permitted at Grotta Resort. All food and beverages must be provided by the resort. This ensures the quality and consistency of your dining experience. Exceptions may apply for special celebration cakes with prior arrangement — please contact our team.

Activities & Experiences

Caves, outdoor sports, wellness, and experiences available at the resort.
Activities at Grotta Resort include: cave exploration (guided walks, adventure routes), use of the swimming pool, gym, outdoor sports area, highland cycling, trail running, yoga sessions, farm experience, and guided property tours. Gorilla trekking packages can also be arranged — Grotta is minutes from Volcanoes National Park.
Some activities such as cave adventure routes, yoga sessions, and guided experiences require advance booking due to limited availability. Pool and gym access are available to all guests at any time. We recommend booking any experiences at least 24–48 hours in advance.
The Scenic Cave Walk (16 minutes, easy) is suitable for most guests including families and older visitors. The Adventure Route (up to 1 hour, moderate) requires some stooping and sturdy footwear, with a maximum of 8 guests. All cave visits are guided by our team. Guests with mobility challenges should speak to our team for personalised advice.
Yes. Our concierge team can assist with arranging gorilla trekking permits and logistics through Volcanoes National Park. Grotta Resort is ideally positioned as a base for gorilla trekking — minutes from the park entrance. Please contact us in advance as permits must be secured early, especially during peak season.

Weddings

Celebrations, receptions, and wedding events at Grotta Resort.
Grotta Resort is not licensed to legally conduct wedding ceremonies. Guests are welcome to host celebrations, receptions, and symbolic ceremonies at the resort. Legal wedding ceremonies must be conducted through the appropriate civil or religious authorities before or after your Grotta celebration. Our team can advise on this process.
Grotta Resort has two ceremony and reception venues: the Highland Panorama (outdoor, up to 150 guests, volcano backdrop) and the Garden Terrace (garden setting, up to 200 guests). Both venues can be configured for ceremonies, receptions, and celebrations.
Outside catering for food is not permitted. All food and beverage at wedding events must be provided by Grotta Resort. External vendors for other services — such as photography, floristry, and entertainment — may be considered subject to prior approval by our events team. A coordination fee may apply.
We accommodate weddings of various sizes. Please contact our wedding team directly to discuss your guest count and requirements. Every celebration at Grotta is bespoke — we do not offer fixed packages with minimum counts.
We recommend booking at least 6–12 months in advance for larger celebrations, especially during peak season (June–September and December–January). Popular dates fill quickly. Contact our wedding team to check availability for your preferred date.
Add-on services such as photography, floristry, entertainment, and fireworks are priced upon request. Pricing depends on the specific service, supplier, and scope of your event. Our wedding team will provide a custom quotation based on your requirements.

Booking & Payments

Reservations, cancellations, payments, and policies.
You can make a reservation directly through our website booking form, by emailing info@grottaresort.rw, or by contacting us via WhatsApp. We recommend booking directly to ensure the best available rate and direct communication with our team.
All room rates are displayed in USD. Payments may be processed in USD or RWF depending on the payment method. Please confirm currency with our reservations team at the time of booking.
Cancellation policies vary depending on the room type, rate, and booking period. Standard bookings typically require cancellation at least 48–72 hours before arrival to avoid a charge. Event bookings, group bookings, and special rate reservations may have different terms. Full details are provided at the time of booking.
Yes. Rates may vary during public holidays, long weekends, festive periods (Christmas, New Year), and high-demand dates. Minimum-stay requirements may also apply during peak periods. Final rates are confirmed at the time of booking.
For standard room bookings, a deposit may be required to confirm your reservation. For events, weddings, and group bookings, a deposit is required and the terms will be outlined in your booking agreement. Please contact our reservations team for specific deposit requirements.
