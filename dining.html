Dining at Grotta

Dining takes place across multiple environments designed to match the rhythm of the day.

Primary Hub

Grotta Café

"The first moment of arrival, where every journey through Grotta begins."

Positioned at the entrance of the cave, the Grotta Café is both the threshold and the heart. It is where guests first arrive, pause, and allow the atmosphere to settle around them — the stone cool, the light low, the pace immediately different.

This is where initial impressions form. Where conversations begin without agenda. Where the decision to go deeper — into the cave, into the evening, into the experience — unfolds at its own natural pace.

Five Environments

From tasting menus inside the cave to slow mornings in the garden, each space at Grotta is designed for a different moment in the day.

✦
Dinner · From 6PM

Restaurant

Fine dining featuring hyper-local ingredients. Experiential tasting menus elevated by modern techniques in a stunning setting overlooking the highlands.

All Day · 10AM–10PM

Cave Café

Intimate, candlelit, serving rare spirits and artisanal tapas deep within the bedrock. A truly unique experience beneath ancient stone.
