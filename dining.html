Dining & Drinks

Three spaces for every moment of the day.

From a refined evening restaurant to a cave-side café and an evening cocktail lounge — Grotta Resort offers distinct dining spaces that feel unlike anything else in the region.

01 The Restaurant

Fine Dining

Where local ingredients meet considered cooking.

Our main restaurant is open for dinner every evening. We work with local farmers and producers from the Musanze highlands to create a seasonal menu that changes with what is available and at its best.

Guests can choose from a full tasting menu or order à la carte. Private dining is available on request.

Dinner only · Open from 6:00 PM · Reservations recommended

02 Cave Café

All-Day Café

Coffee, drinks and bites at the cave entrance.

The Cave Café sits at the entrance to Grotta's natural cave system. It is open throughout the day for specialty coffee, hot drinks, light meals, artisanal snacks, and an evening selection of rare spirits.

No reservation needed. The café is open to all guests at any time during operating hours.

Open Daily · 10:00 AM – 10:00 PM · No reservation required

03 Cocktail Bar & Lounge

Evening Lounge

Crafted cocktails as the highland evening settles in.

Open every evening, our cocktail bar and lounge is a comfortable space to wind down after a day at the resort. The cocktail list draws on local botanicals and Rwandan spirits.

Wines by the glass, bar snacks, and a relaxed setting. Open to all guests from 5PM.

Open Daily · From 5:00 PM · Open to all guests

Relaxation

Pool and gardens for every part of your stay.

The swimming pool and outdoor gardens are available to all guests throughout the day. Both spaces are included with every room and require no reservation.

04 Swimming Pool

The Pool

Open every day, included with every room.

The resort swimming pool is open daily and available to all guests at no extra charge. It sits within the resort grounds with views of the surrounding highland landscape.

Towels are provided. No booking required.

05 Outdoor Gardens & Terraces

The Gardens

Landscaped grounds open throughout the day and evening.

The resort gardens and terraces surround the property and are open to all guests. Walk through the farm garden, sit on the highland terraces, or gather at the evening bonfire.

The gardens are also used for outdoor dining and private events during warmer months.

Always Open · Included with all rooms
