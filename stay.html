The Grotta Difference

Why Guests Choose
Grotta Resort

01
Altitude & Silence

Musanze's highland altitude creates a particular quality of quiet that most guests describe as transformative. It begins before you unpack.

02
Volcanic Landscape

Every room is positioned to engage with the volcanic highland landscape from private terraces to floor-to-ceiling windows facing ancient peaks.

03
No Compromise

From the Standard Room to the Executive Suite each category is designed around the complete experience of its guest, with nothing withheld.

04
Cave & Wellness

Ancient volcanic caves, highland jacuzzi, sauna, and yoga a wellness environment that uses the landscape itself as part of the restoration ritual.

05
Family & Groups

From the Family Room's farm experience and movie room to the Executive's butler service every type of guest finds something designed specifically for them.

06
Gorilla Country

Minutes from Volcanoes National Park. Grotta Resort offers the most refined base for gorilla trekking in Rwanda comfort before and recovery after.
