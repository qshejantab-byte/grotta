01 Heat & Stone

Volcanic Sauna

Heated by volcanic stone in a space designed for stillness. The dry heat settles around you slowly, and the silence here is the kind that asks nothing of you. An hour inside changes the quality of the day.

02 Water & Sky

Outdoor Jacuzzi

Open to the highland air, the jacuzzi faces the volcanic landscape. Steam rises against the cooler temperature of altitude. An evening here — under open sky — becomes the kind of memory guests return for.

03 Rest & Reflection

Pool

A serene pool set within the highland grounds. The water is still most mornings. Afternoons bring light that shifts across the surface in a way that invites you to stop thinking and simply be present.

04 Movement

Gym & Training

A thoughtfully equipped space for movement at any pace. The highland altitude adds an edge to every session. Floor-to-ceiling windows open toward the volcano peaks.

05 Coming Soon

Cave Spa
