August 3–6 · 4 Days · 18 Guests Maximum

Four days held entirely within the volcanic highland environment of Grotta. No programme to follow. No itinerary to honour. Just a setting that asks something of you — to slow down, to be present, to allow the landscape to do what landscapes do when you finally let them.

Mornings that begin before sunrise. Communal meals prepared with highland ingredients. Wellness rituals designed for the altitude. Evenings around fire. Sleep unlike any sleep you have had recently.
